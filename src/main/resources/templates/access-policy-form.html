How Access Policies Work
An access policy is a reusable set of permissions for your employees. After creating the policy, you can configure applications and websites that employees assigned to this policy can access.

Create the policy Give your policy a meaningful name and description.

Configure websites Control access to websites and domains.
4
Assign employees Apply the policy to one or more employees.
New policies are active by default
